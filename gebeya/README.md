# Gebeya Watch (ገበያ)
A problem-solving web app for Ethiopian students and families. Prices change fast and are hard to compare. Users report the prices they pay (teff, onions, oil, sugar, eggs, milk, fuel) in ETB by city and market; the app shows the cheapest city, weekly trends and price rises, and a **student budget planner** tests a monthly allowance against current prices.
